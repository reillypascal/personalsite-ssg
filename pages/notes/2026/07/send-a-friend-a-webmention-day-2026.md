Today is [Send a Friend a Webmention Day](https://events.indieweb.org/2026/07/send-a-friend-a-webmention-day-W4tImKtqUGsA)! [Webmentions](https://indieweb.org/Webmention) are a way to make posting on your own website more social, and I think they're pretty neat.

I have [a page](/wiki/tutorials/webmention-tutorial/) with a bunch of info about setting them up on your site — the basics are pretty straightforward!

Today I'm mentioning [Joe](https://artlung.com/), [Naty](https://burgeonlab.com/), [Ross](https://rossabaker.com/), and [Jo](https://dead.garden/). They all have cool websites you should check out if you haven't already!

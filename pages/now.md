<h2 class="sectionHeader">Current Activities</h2>

I've continued to update my [data2audio](/code/#data2audio) Rust CLI tool for converting any file into audio. The tool has made it *much* easier to dig through my computer's drive for files that will create interesting sounds, and this has in turn made my current composition project easier.

I've slowly started to get back into reading fiction, and looking over the past year or so, I realized I actually read a fair amount. There's a bookstore right at the end of my street that also hosts community events, and I've started going to their &ldquo;weird books club&rdquo; as well. It's also been helping to accept that I like to be in the middle of a long list of books at any given time, and to freely jump between them.

{%- comment %}
I recently wrote [my first Neovim plugin](https://codeberg.org/reillypascal/algorave.nvim), an REPL primarily aimed at musical live-coding, or “algorave” performances, but flexible enough to cover other use cases. It was a challenge at first, but very satisfying to figure out, and it's been useful too. In addition to using it with Tidal Cycles, it's been useful to set up the [Lilypond `scheme-sandbox` command](https://lilypond.org/doc/v2.26/Documentation/extending/scheme-sandbox) to let me learn Scheme in the context of [extending Lilypond](https://extending-lilypond.gitlab.io/en/index.html).

Speaking of which, I've been [learning Lilypond](/tags/lilypond/) and I'm doing my current composition project in it! I started looking into it when Finale was discontinued, and as I've grown to like Neovim more, and now prefer that workflow to many GUI ones, I decided to give Lilypond another go. It's been very nice (if a bit of a steep learning curve), and it's been helping me get back into writing music on the page, after a long spell of focusing on sound design with Max/MSP and various code projects.

I've come to appreciate older email etiquette (e.g., replies below quoted text), and I've also been pleased to discover that (I guess unsurprisingly) people still email like that on the [lilypond-user mailing list](https://lists.gnu.org/mailman/listinfo/lilypond-user).

I've been wanting to learn how to write externals for Max/MSP in C for a while now, and I finally got around to learning how. I have [one started](https://github.com/reillypascal/rs.max), and it's turning out to be a lot of fun.

[Writing for the September 2025 IndieWeb Carnival](/posts/2025/09/september-indieweb-carnival/) got me excited about creative writing and fiction. I've added a [fiction page](/fiction) to my site. I've been reading some Borges and thinking about metafiction, and that's giving me some fun ideas I think I'd like to try.

Recently I've found some new hobbies. First is photography. I tracked down an analog SLR camera (a Minolta X-370) that I had in high school but never used, and I'm learning analog photography with it. I also found some local classes on developing black & white film, and that should be a fun skill to learn.
